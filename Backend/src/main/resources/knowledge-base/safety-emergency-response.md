# Emergency Response & Helpline Numbers (India)

What to do in an active emergency, and verified national helpline numbers.
These are India-wide numbers; always also note your local police station
and nearest hospital for your area.

## If you are in immediate danger right now

1. Call 112 (India's unified national emergency number — connects to
   police, fire, and medical/ambulance services) or your local police
   directly if you can.
2. If you can't speak safely, most phones let you trigger an emergency
   SOS by pressing the power button three times quickly, or via the 112
   India app's "SHOUT" feature, which alerts nearby registered volunteers
   as well as the emergency response centre.
3. Trigger UnSocial's SOS Alert so your own emergency contacts get your
   live location immediately too — this works alongside calling 112, not
   instead of it.
4. Get to a public, populated, well-lit place if you're able to move
   safely.

UnSocial's SOS Alert notifies the people you've personally chosen and
trust. It does not contact police or government emergency services
automatically — for that, you still need to call 112 (or your local
equivalent) directly.

## National helpline numbers (India)

- **112** — National unified emergency number (police, fire, medical,
  disaster response). India's Supreme Court has directed all states to
  fully operationalise 112 as the single number for every emergency, so
  it's the most reliable number to remember.
- **100** — Police (being merged into 112, but still active in most
  areas)
- **101** — Fire
- **102 / 108** — Ambulance
- **1091** — Women's Helpline
- **181** — Women Helpline (24/7, for women facing violence or distress
  of any kind, including domestic violence — run under the Ministry of
  Women & Child Development's Universalization of Women Helpline scheme)
- **1098** — Child Helpline
- **14567** — Senior Citizen Helpline
- **14490 / 7827170170** — National Commission for Women (NCW) 24x7
  helpline — counseling, legal guidance, and complaint registration for
  domestic violence, sexual harassment, and related issues

Numbers and rollout details can vary slightly by state during this
transition to the unified 112 system — if in doubt, 112 is always a safe
default.

## After an immediate threat has passed

Note down details while they're fresh: time, location, description of
anyone involved, vehicle numbers, and any witnesses. If you're physically
hurt, get medical attention even if injuries seem minor — some injuries
and the resulting medical record both matter later. If you want to file a
police report, you can do so at any police station regardless of where the
incident occurred (a "Zero FIR"); you're entitled to a free copy of it.
None of this is legal advice — for anything beyond general information,
a lawyer, the NCW helpline, or your local women's helpline (181) can guide
you through next steps specific to your situation.

Timer for situations with a known time window (a date, a solo trip, a
